Task 5: Controlled Generation via Classifier-Free Guidance

1. Introduction

Maintaining high semantic quality in low-data regimes is a persistent challenge for discrete diffusion. This task implements Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) to steer the Encoder-Decoder model by interpolating between standard conditional logits and gradients from a secondary quality predictor.

2. Implementation Details

A 3-layer Quality Classifier (139.14K parameters) is trained to estimate target BERTScore from intermediate hidden states. During inference, we modify the predicted logits by a guidance scale λ: z' = z + λ · ∇hQ(h).

👉 Implementation: analysis/quality_classifier.py

3. Experimental Observations

The guidance sweep demonstrated that for the Encoder-Decoder variant, the optimal guidance scale is **λ = 0.0**. Increasing guidance beyond 1.0 introduced semantic "hallucinations"—where the model generated valid but incorrect Sanskrit tokens due to noisy gradients from the small classifier dataset (N<100).

4. Analysis & Conclusion

While the CFG framework is mathematically integrated, its effectiveness is bottlenecked by the classifier's training data volume. For the Encoder-Decoder architecture, the T4 denoising loop provides sufficient baseline quality, and additional steering currently introduces more noise than refinement. Future scaling of the quality-labeled corpus remains a prerequisite for enabling non-zero guidance.
